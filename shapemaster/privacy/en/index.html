Shape Master Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Controller: Franco Vilardo

Contact: favilardo@gmail.com

1. What Shape Master is

Shape Master ("the game") is an arcade game for Android. This policy explains what data the game handles, for what purpose, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

In short:

  • The game has no accounts and no servers of its own. The developer does not receive or store your gameplay data.
  • Signing in with Google is optional and only serves to save your progress in the cloud of your own Google account.
  • The game shows Google AdMob ads, which may process data such as the advertising ID.

2. Data stored on your device

The game keeps a save file in the app's private storage (not accessible to other apps) with:

  • Your progress: best Endless score, completed levels and stars.
  • Your inventory: coins, in-game unlocks (skins, themes, powers, upgrades) and the power equipped on each side. Coins are a virtual in-game currency; they cannot be bought with real money.
  • Preferences: music and sound on or off, equipped skin and theme.
  • Device progress: daily missions, daily streak, ads watched to unlock skins, and the date of the last "revive with coins".
  • A random installation identifier (generated by the game itself, unrelated to your device, your account or your advertising ID) used to tell which device earned which coins when progress is merged between devices.

This data stays on your device and is used only to make the game work. It leaves the device only if you sign in with Google (section 3).

3. Google sign-in and cloud save (optional)

You can tap "Sign in with Google" to save and restore your progress across devices. It is not required to play.

Permissions requested from Google (OAuth scopes): openid, email and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata.

  • drive.appdata gives access only to this app's hidden data folder in your Google Drive. The game cannot see or modify your other Drive files.
  • From your Google account the game only uses your email address, and only to display it in the main menu while you are signed in. It is not sent to the developer or saved in the cloud. The game does not request your name or profile photo.
  • The game stores on your device the access and refresh tokens that Google issues, so it does not ask you to sign in every time. They are stored encrypted with the Android Keystore and deleted when you sign out.
  • During sign-in, Google sends you back to the app through the address https://favilardo.github.io/oauth2redirect, hosted on GitHub Pages. Android opens the game directly with that address, without loading any page, and the game does not keep the authorization code after exchanging it. If your device did not open the game and the browser loaded that address instead, GitHub would receive the request like any web server and handle it under its own policy: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

What is uploaded to your Google Drive (file savegame.json in the app's hidden folder): best score, completed levels, stars, counters of coins earned and spent (per installation identifier), purchased items, upgrade levels, equipped powers and the date of that change. Not uploaded: your email, the equipped skin or theme, sound preferences, missions or streak.

When it syncs: when you sign in, and when you open the app while signed in, the file is downloaded and merged with your local progress; afterwards, every time the game saves, the updated version is uploaded. Data travels over HTTPS directly between your device and Google. The developer has no access to that file.

Google handles that data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows Google AdMob ads: a banner in the menus, full-screen ads after a number of levels or defeats, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for coins, a revive or a skin). The game does not show ads during gameplay.

AdMob is a Google service that, in order to show and measure ads, may automatically collect and process data such as: your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and system information, ad interactions and diagnostic data. The developer does not receive that personal data, only aggregated revenue reports. The exact list and purposes are defined by Google; see https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

  • Advertising ID: the app declares Android's advertising ID permission. You can reset it or turn off ad personalization in Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Consent: the game integrates Google's consent form (UMP). In regions where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you will be shown a form to choose your preferences before ads load.
  • Ad type: the game sets a maximum ad content rating of "G" (suitable for all audiences). The child-directed treatment flag is not set (see section 8).

5. Sharing your record

The "Share" button generates, on your device, an image with your score or level and the equipped skin, and hands it to Android's share menu. You choose which app to share it with. The game does not upload the image to any server of its own; once shared, it is handled by the app you choose, under its own policy. The image does not include your name, email or other personal data.

6. Who the data is shared with

  • Google (Google Sign-In, Google Drive and AdMob), as described above.
  • Hosting of the sign-in redirect page: GitHub Pages (see section 3).
  • There are no other third parties: the game's code includes no analytics tools of its own or third-party crash reporting, and no real-money purchases. Google's ads library (AdMob) does collect diagnostic and ad-usage data on its own, as described in section 4.
  • We do not sell personal data.

7. Retention and how to delete your data

  • Data on the device: removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data (Android Settings > Apps > Shape Master > Storage > Clear data).
  • Signing out: the "Sign out" button in the menu deletes the Google tokens from the device and stops syncing. It does not delete the file already stored in your Google Drive.
  • Deleting the cloud copy: in Google Drive (web version) open Settings > Manage apps, find Shape Master and, in its options, choose to delete hidden app data. These option names are defined by Google and may change.
  • Removing access: in your Google account, "Security > Your connections to third-party apps and services" (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), choose Shape Master and "Remove access".
  • Ads: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. To delete data Google already holds because of ads, use your Google account controls ("My Activity").
  • Since the developer does not store your data on servers of its own, there is no developer database from which you need to be deleted. If you have questions, write to favilardo@gmail.com.

8. Children

Shape Master is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Communications with Google (sign-in, Drive and ads) use HTTPS. The local save file is in the app's private storage. No system is 100% secure.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example GDPR/LGPD or other laws) you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection or portability. Since gameplay data is on your device and in your own Google Drive, you can exercise most of those rights directly (sections 3 and 7). For other requests write to favilardo@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change what data the game handles, we will update this page and the date above.
