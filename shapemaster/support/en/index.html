Shape Master Support

What Shape Master is

Shape Master is an arcade game for Android and iPhone, developed by Franco Vilardo.

How to get help or report a problem

Write to favilardo@gmail.com and include:

  • Your phone model.
  • Your system version (Android or iOS).
  • The game version, shown in Settings inside the game.
  • A description of what happened and, if you can, a screenshot.

Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved on iPhone?

Yes, but only on your device. On iPhone there is no sign-in or cloud save, so your progress is lost if you delete the app.

Can I save my progress to the cloud on Android?

Yes. On Android you can optionally sign in with Google to save your progress to your own Google Drive and restore it on another device. It is not required to play.

How do I change my choice about personalized ads on iPhone?

In iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking you can allow or deny tracking for Shape Master at any time.

Does the game have real-money purchases?

No. Coins are a virtual in-game currency and cannot be bought with real money.
